Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18.

Overview

AFG Trinity Construction ERP ("the Software") is a private internal tool used only by AFG Trinity Construction LLC. It does not collect, transmit, or share data with any third party.

Data we store

All data — clients, jobs, financial records, partner and supplier records — is stored in a local SQLite database on the workstation where the Software runs. No data is sent to remote servers operated by AFG Trinity Construction LLC.

QuickBooks Online

If connected to QuickBooks Online, the Software uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 flow to read transaction data on the user's behalf. OAuth tokens are stored locally and never shared. The Software does not transmit QuickBooks data anywhere outside the user's machine.

Cookies / analytics

None.
